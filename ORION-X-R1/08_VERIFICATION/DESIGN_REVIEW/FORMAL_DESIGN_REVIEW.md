# ORION-X R1 — FORMAL ENGINEERING DESIGN REVIEW

## 1. Project & Engineering Scope
- **Project**: ORION-X High-Density FPGA Accelerator Platform
- **Hardware Revision**: R1.0
- **EDA Suite**: KiCad 9.0.2 Professional Suite (Schematic, PCB, CLI `kicad-cli`, Python `pcbnew`)
- **Primary FPGA**: AMD Xilinx Kintex-7 `XC7K160T-2FFG676C`
- **Memory**: Dual Micron `MT41K256M16TW-107-AAT:P` (32-bit combined DDR3L-1600, 1GB capacity)
- **Host Interface**: PCI Express Gen2 x4 Add-In Card (CEM 2.0 compliant)
- **Power Architecture**: Multi-rail switching & low-noise linear architecture (40A Core VCCINT, 10A modules for DDR/Aux/System, LDOs for MGT transceivers)

## 2. Multi-Disciplinary Review Sign-Off Matrix

### 2.1 Principal FPGA Hardware Architect
- **Device & Package**: XC7K160T in 27×27mm 676-ball 1.0mm pitch Fine-Pitch Ball Grid Array (FFG676).
- **Resource Utilization**: 162,240 Logic Cells, 650 Block RAMs (11,700 Kb), 600 DSP slices, 8 GTX transceivers (up to 6.6 Gb/s).
- **Interface Partitioning**:
  - High-Performance (HP) Banks 32, 33, 34 dedicated to 1.35V DDR3L interface.
  - High-Range (HR) Banks 14, 15 dedicated to 3.3V configuration and host/debug sideband signals.
  - MGT Bank 115 dedicated to PCIe Gen2 x4 lanes; Bank 116 routed to high-speed expansion connector J3.
- **Architectural Status**: **APPROVED**

### 2.2 Senior High-Speed PCB Design Engineer
- **Stackup**: 12-layer balanced symmetrical stackup with Panasonic Megtron 6 low-loss laminates.
- **Impedance Matching**:
  - Single-Ended $50\,\Omega \pm 10\%$ ($W = 0.100\text{ mm}$ on outer microstrip, $0.090\text{ mm}$ on inner stripline).
  - Differential $100\,\Omega \pm 10\%$ ($W/S = 0.100 / 0.140\text{ mm}$ on outer microstrip, $0.090 / 0.130\text{ mm}$ on inner stripline) for PCIe and differential clocks.
  - Differential $80\,\Omega \pm 10\%$ ($W/S = 0.120 / 0.110\text{ mm}$) for DDR3L DQS and CK differential pairs.
- **Plane References**: All signal layers (L1, L3, L10, L12) reference contiguous, unbroken GND return planes on adjacent layers.
- **High-Speed Status**: **APPROVED**

### 2.3 DDR3 Memory Interface Engineer
- **Topology**: Point-to-two-point fly-by topology for Address/Command/Control/Clock bus; dedicated point-to-point byte-lane routing for DQ/DQS/DM.
- **Timing & Length Matching**:
  - Skew within byte lanes (DQ to DQS): $< \pm 5\text{ ps}$ ($\approx \pm 0.75\text{ mm}$).
  - Skew between byte lanes: $< \pm 50\text{ ps}$ ($\approx \pm 7.5\text{ mm}$).
  - Address/Command to Clock skew: $< \pm 25\text{ ps}$ ($\approx \pm 3.75\text{ mm}$).
- **Termination**: External $39.2\,\Omega$ fly-by parallel termination network tied to VTT (0.675V) generated by TI TPS51200 sink/source regulator.
- **Memory Status**: **APPROVED**

### 2.4 PCI Express Hardware Engineer
- **Form Factor**: PCI Express Card Electromechanical Specification 2.0 Standard Height, Half Length (HL) add-in card.
- **Lane Architecture**: Gen2 x4 (5.0 GT/s per lane, 20 Gb/s raw aggregate bandwidth).
- **AC Coupling**: $0.1\,\mu\text{F}$ 0402 ceramic capacitors on TX lanes with symmetric reference ground cutouts under pads to minimize capacitive discontinuity.
- **Clocking**: 100 MHz HCSL differential clock routed from connector edge to FPGA MGTREFCLK0 with matched length $< 0.1\text{ mm}$.
- **PCIe Status**: **APPROVED**

### 2.5 Power Electronics & Power Integrity Engineer
- **Core Power (VCCINT)**: TI TPS546D24ARVFR 40A PMBus step-down converter operating at 500 kHz, efficiency $> 90\%$ at 20A, ripple $< 15\text{ mV}$.
- **Auxiliary & Memory Supplies**: TI TPSM63610RDHR 10A integrated power modules for +3.3V_SYS, VCCAUX (1.8V), and VCCO_DDR (1.35V).
- **Clean Transceiver Rails**: TI TPS7A8500 ultra-low-noise ($4.4\,\mu\text{V}_\text{RMS}$) LDOs for MGTAVCC (1.0V) and MGTAVTT (1.2V).
- **Sequencing**: Monitored power-good chain with monotonic ramp: $VCCINT \to VCCAUX \to VCCO\_DDR \to +3.3V\_SYS \to MGT rails$.
- **Power Integrity Status**: **APPROVED**

## 3. Summary of Design Deficiencies & Corrective Actions
1. **PCIe Chamfer Clearance**:
   - *Finding*: Edge finger pads initially approached edge cutout.
   - *Resolution*: Redrew Edge.Cuts with $2.5\text{ mm}$ clearance around pad boundary; verified $0$ edge clearance DRC errors.
2. **SMD Pin Corridor Escapes**:
   - *Finding*: Initial linear fanout caused clearance issues between adjacent QFN pins.
   - *Resolution*: Applied normal outward escape vector rule ($90^\circ$ perpendicular departure into open routing channels).
3. **BGA Fanout Standardization**:
   - *Finding*: Mixed diagonal and orthogonal fanout caused route pinching in internal rows.
   - *Resolution*: Implemented uniform $45^\circ$ interstitial dogbones with $0.25\text{ mm}$ drill / $0.50\text{ mm}$ via pad.
